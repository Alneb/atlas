# Code of conduct

Atlas follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/): be kind and constructive, assume good faith, and keep criticism on the work. Harassment, insults and personal or political attacks are not accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers, through the contact on the repository's profile. Every report is read and kept confidential. The maintainers may warn, suspend or ban, as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
